    How to label

    1. Open the listing with the green button and read the whole page: the table, the ticked features and the description.
    2. Label what the listing says. Blank means it doesn't say. Never fill a gap with a likely value.
    3. "table" values come from our copy of the page's table on 20 October. If the live page shows something else because the listing changed (a new price, say), keep our value, pick "changed since 20 October" and note what changed. If the listing is gone, pick "gone" and save.
    4. When the listing contradicts itself (table against text), leave the field blank and note both values. Except price and size: the table wins.
    5. A missing tick is not a no. Lift, balcony or storage are "no" only when the text says so.
    6. Lift is "no" if it stops below the flat's floor, or it's a goods lift (αναβατόριο).
    7. Floor: υπερυψωμένο ισόγειο is raised ground. A maisonette takes its lowest level. A ρετιρέ with a floor number takes the number.
    8. Bathrooms are full bathrooms only. A WC is not a bathroom.
    9. Energy: Γ is C, Ζ is F, Η is G. Α++ is A+. Εξαιρείται is exempt. Υπό έκδοση is pending.
    10. Under construction: the year built is the delivery year, and the condition is new build.
    11. Parking: pilotis (πιλοτή), garage (closed or underground), space (an open or covered spot). A tick with no type in the text stays blank.
    12. Metro minutes only when minutes or metres are stated: metres ÷ 80, rounded up.
    13. Never copy a name, phone or email into evidence or notes.
