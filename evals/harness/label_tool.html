Notes
Blank means unknown. "table" marks a value code read from the page: check it against the text. Select words in the listing, then press ❝ to keep them as evidence. Ctrl or ⌘ + Enter: done, next. Alt + ↑ ↓: move between listings.

The rules that come up most

  1. Label what the listing says, anywhere on the page. Blank is unknown. Never fill a gap with a likely value.
  2. Conflicts are unknown. If the table or ticks and the text clearly disagree, leave the field blank and note both values. Two exceptions: the price and the size, where the table wins (note the text's figure).
  3. A missing tick is not a no. Set lift, balcony or storage to "no" only when the text says so.
  4. Lift: "no" if the text says the lift stops below the home's floor, or there is only a goods lift (αναβατόριο).
  5. Floor: υπερυψωμένο ισόγειο is raised ground even when the table says Ισόγειο. A maisonette takes its lowest level. A ρετιρέ with a floor number takes the number; "top" is only for a ρετιρέ with no number.
  6. Bathrooms count full bathrooms only. A WC is not a bathroom.
  7. Energy: Α++ is A+. Εξαιρείται is exempt. Υπό έκδοση or σε εξέλιξη is pending.
  8. Under construction: the year built is the stated delivery year, and the condition is new_build.
  9. Parking: pilotis (πιλοτή), garage (closed or underground), space (open or covered spot), none. A tick with no type in the text stays blank.
  10. Metro minutes only when minutes or metres are stated. Metres ÷ 80, rounded up.
  11. Never copy a name, phone or email into evidence or notes.
